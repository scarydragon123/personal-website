The Case of the Missing Children

A journey into the mysterious world of Neltopia

Uncover the secrets hidden in the enchanted forests of a world beyond imagination.

The Case of the Missing Children

Chapter One: The Encounter with the Mysterious Man

Day after day, we're stuck in an ominous routine, haunted by a mysterious figure in a black suit with a blood-red tie. His deep, scary voice fills the air, making everything feel dark and tense. We can't shake the fear that we could be his next victim, but we never know who it'll be and we can't help but wonder who he's going to target next.

The man walks into the room, bringing a dark, heavy feeling with him. His voice is full of despair as he commands, "ELLA HERE NOW!" His words send a chill down our spines. "Uh uh, no one else, please," Mr. Osborne stammers, clearly terrified. The disappointment in the room is apparent because Ella, who's usually so friendly and quiet, is singled out. Everyone's worried because even though Ella keeps to herself, even she is not safe from this ominous figure's routine.

Ella is shaking with fear, tears running down her face. She stammers, "Where are you t-taking me, LOSS?" Her voice is full of fear, begging for answers, but the mysterious man doesn't care. He just laughs cruelly and says, "HAHA, YOU WILL NEVER SEE THIS SCHOOL AGAIN!" His mocking words echo down the dark hallway. Then, he pulls out a syringe filled with a glowing purple liquid, and in the darkness, it's the only thing glowing, lighting up the dark surroundings. As he injects the liquid, the room glows for a moment, contrasting with the deep fear Ella feels. What happens next is a mystery as she's plunged into the unknown.

Chapter Two: Axel's Experiences

Day after day, peculiar things kept happening to me. One day, while I was out jogging, my legs started moving on their own, like something was controlling them, and to my shock, they were on fire! I told Isabella about it the next day, and to my surprise, she was going through strange experiences too. The fact that we were both experiencing these strange events made me feel uneasy, like something big was going on.

When I got home, I felt a weird sensation, like something was calling me but from inside. I ignored it and went to bed, but there I found a strange crystal ball. There was a clean, white note next to it that said, "Dear Axel, tell no one but one, for this ball holds great power — it can reveal anything by just the saying of a name."

I thought hard about who I could trust and finally decided on Isabella. She was the only person I could rely on to understand. When I told her about the crystal ball, she was skeptical, but when she tried it out, its powers were real. We were both astounded. The next day, we got another note that said, "Isabella and Axel, meet on the second moon of the month; you two are siblings."

The idea that we might be siblings was confusing and raised a lot of questions. As we tried to figure out what this meant, the crystal ball became a tool to uncover secrets we never imagined.

Chapter Three: The Journey to the Tree

As night fell, our curiosity was too strong to ignore. We needed to uncover the mysteries around us. Moving quickly to avoid being seen, we approached the old oak tree that stood out in the dim light.

A mysterious man appeared, wearing a black coat with a symbol on his cloak. His face was hidden, adding to the mystery. Then, with a voice that felt like pins in my ears, he told us to follow him. Scared but curious, we did. As if answering some kind of magic command, the tree shook violently and fell over. Underneath, we found a hidden ladder leading down into the unknown.

We climbed down and saw something amazing—the man descended, and we followed, with the tree somehow standing back up to cover the entrance behind us. "Welcome," the man said, revealing the name of this strange place—NELTOPIA. The world around us was incredible, filled with lush plants, bright flowers, and a community of people living in houses of all shapes and sizes. The energy of this hidden paradise was electric, leaving us in awe of what lay ahead.

Chapter Four: Ella's Strange New World

Ella suddenly wakes up in a strange place, feeling confused and in pain. She struggles to remember what happened, realizing with shock that she can't remember falling asleep. She lets out a small scream as the harsh reality hits her, but for a moment, she can't believe it's real. As the truth sinks in, she starts to accept that her life has completely changed, and a wave of emotions hits her hard.

Looking around, Ella sees a dark, eerie room. The only light comes from a flickering flame in the corner. She's alone, but a creeping feeling warns her she's being watched.

Chapter Five: The Challenge Begins

"What's the twist?"

Ella asks with wide eyes as Master Loss appears, his dark figure looming over her.

Master Loss chuckles, "Well, let me explain. You have to participate in a race, but not just any race." His voice lowers menacingly. "If you manage to cross the starting line, you'll be racing against one of my creatures: Dignity, Hope, or Death. Choose wisely," he sneers. "Each of them is deadly in their own way, and if any of them catch you, they are free to kill."

Ella's heart races as she listens to his words, realizing her life depends on her choice.

"I choose Hope,"

she says, her voice barely a whisper, trying to summon any courage she has left.

Master Loss smiles cruelly. "Very well," he says, muttering some strange words. A hideous creature materializes in front of her, a monster with three mouths and three arms on each side, its heads swiveling in all directions at once.

Master Loss counts down, "3… 2… 1… go!"

Ella bolts, her legs moving faster than she ever thought possible. She feels the creature's hot breath close behind, but desperation fuels her speed. She pushes herself to the limit, her mind focused solely on escaping. With every ounce of energy, she manages to stay just ahead, running until she finally outruns the creature, her heart still pounding with fear.

Chapter Six: The Cruel Revelation

Just as Ella starts to catch her breath, Master Loss taunts her further. "Haha! I have some rather sad news. Your parents are dead."

He laughs as Ella's heart drops. She screams in agony, and up ahead, she spots something that makes her blood run cold—her parents' bodies are being devoured by hideous creatures in the distance.

"I have to get through this," she whispers to herself, fighting the anger. Despite everything, she manages to push forward, feeling the weight of her loss driving her forward, giving her a grim sense of purpose.

Chapter Seven: The Final Test

Her heart is pounding, the noise of her own footsteps echoing in her ears. Master Loss is laughing somewhere in the shadows, but then, everything goes silent.

"You've passed the first test, but there's one more," Master Loss sneers, stepping out of the darkness. He explains, "You must race again, but this time against my strongest creature, Death itself. If you succeed, you will live. If you fail, there will be no second chance."

Ella's heart skips a beat. She realizes that this final race will determine everything.

With all her might, Ella sprints forward, pushing through the darkness and pain, knowing this is her last shot at freedom.

The Journey to Neltopia

Suddenly, two rabbits popped out of their homes underground. "These," the mysterious man said, "are talking rabbits—Phil and Jacob. They will take you to the capital city of Neltopia, called Aldenville. There you will meet Liodon, one of the leaders of our economy. He will explain everything." With that, the man walked away, and as he was leaving, he looked over his shoulder and shouted, "Phil, Jacob, take them to Liodon!" As he turned around I could have sworn I saw a smirk on his face.

The rabbits turned to us and said, "Follow us—Liodon awaits."

We had been walking for ages when we finally reached a massive building. It looked like a square with a cylinder coming out of it; at the top of the cylinder was a huge dome. The doors were enormous. The rabbits unlocked the door by scanning their retinas (the colored part of their eyes), and the doors swung open as if by magic.

The foyer was huge. At the front desk sat a small creature that looked like a garden gnome. "Hello Phil, hello Jacob," the gnome said. "These two must be Isabella and Axel—off to see Liodon, I suppose?" he asked with a grin.

"Yes," said Phil… or was it Jacob? They looked so alike. "Well, off you go then," said the garden gnome. He opened a door for us, which led into a hallway, and at the end was an elevator. We clambered in, and it took us right to the very top. Suddenly, there was another bold-looking door. It opened to reveal a domed room with bookshelves lining the walls. On one side, sat a tall man with grey hair and a long beard. He was sitting at a grand oak desk.

Meeting Liodon

"Greetings, Isabella and Axel. I'm sure you are wondering who you really are," said Liodon with a knowing smile. "Yes," we said in unison. "We got the notes, and so we were wondering what you meant when you said we were siblings."

"Well," said Liodon, "you two were genetically made and planted into your human parents, who have dedicated their lives to helping the various species that live here in Neltopia."

Meeting our genetic parents

Later that day, we went to meet our genetic parents, Chris and Lexi.

"Hallo," said Lexi in a strong American accent.

"Good day," said Chris. To my relief, they seemed like nice people.

The Home Hub: Entering the Heart of security

After breakfast Lexi took us to Chris's office, which according to her was the mind of the home, basically where the house is run, technology wise. Inside the room, which was behind a thick steel door, there were so many computer screens and TVs every one with different cameras and statistics about the house.

"This," said Chris with a dramatic pause, "is called the HOME HUB. It is what runs the house."

He was clearly quite an enthusiast for security. "Yes please," we said. He showed us everything: the cameras, the entrance gate, the A.I monitoring system. It was so cool, I must be honest I was starting to enjoy Chris more and more.
