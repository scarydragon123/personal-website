Prologue

R

unning, running as fast as I could I couldn't stop. I could hear Alexa behind me. Her breathing indicated that she was slowing down.

"Alexa!" I screamed. "We can't stop! Keep going!"

It started off as a normal day little did I know it wouldn't end that way!

Hi, my name is Bob. I live in Sperelle, a town in the Middle East where there are lots of mountains and mines. Most of the mines are unused, but some the locals use for fishing.

Fishing for a rare ore called tompsprute.

We fishers are called Avers. Our people, the Sperelle, are the last of our kind, or at least we think! We wouldn't know. The city has stopped sending explorers past the border.

They say it's too dangerous.

The borders are these huge pylons with giant electrified cables. Although they are never on as we are running out of coal, we still have to be wary near the fence just in case.
