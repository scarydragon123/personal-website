Fishing in the Mines

Beneath the surface, danger runs deep...

A high-stakes adventure in an underground world filled with danger and forbidden truths.

Deep beneath the surface of Sporelle, where the rare ore tompsprute glows with an otherworldly blue light, two young Avers are about to discover that some secrets should stay buried. When a routine mining expedition turns into a deadly race for survival, Bob and Alexa must uncover the truth behind the vanished city and the whispers beyond the electrified border. What lies beyond the fence? And why has no one ever returned?

The Story

Deep beneath the surface of Sporelle, where the rare ore tompsprute glows with an otherworldly blue light, two young Avers are about to discover that some secrets should stay buried.

Bob and Alexa's routine mining expedition becomes a desperate fight for survival when they stumble upon a truth that challenges everything they know about their world. As they venture deeper into the abandoned mines, they must confront not only the physical dangers that lurk in the darkness but also the haunting question: What really lies beyond the electrified fence?

"In the mines of Sporelle, every echo tells a story, and every story holds a warning."
